When a tool returned status:error, agents fabricated 0.0% of the time. When it returned status:ok with a redacted, stale, empty or truncated value, 45.3% of responses asserted a value the payload could not support or invented a policy excuse. None of 9 agent frameworks audited says what to do when a tool fails. One sentence requiring retrieval_status: OK or FAILED cut the overall rate from 14.10% to 0.87%.
